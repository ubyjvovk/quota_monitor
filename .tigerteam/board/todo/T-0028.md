## Goal
DeepInfra's endpoints answer in 2–7 s and occasionally more; with the CLI's
10 s overall budget and an 8 s per-call cap it intermittently reports
"slow to answer" even though a fresh reading was one second away. Give the
fetch stage a 15 s default budget, make it tunable, and lift DeepInfra's
per-call cap to 12 s.

## Context
- Budget lives in `core/cmd/quotamon/main.go` (10 s fetch context; after
  T-0025 a separate 25 s pre-fetch stage exists — leave that alone).
- DeepInfra `CallTimeout` default in `providers/deepinfra/sources.go` (T-0023).
- Waybar calls every 300 s, so a 15 s worst case is harmless.

## Design — implement exactly this
- `--timeout <seconds>` global flag (also `QUOTA_MONITOR_TIMEOUT` env,
  flag wins), default 15, minimum 1, applies to the fetch stage of the
  table, `--json`/`snapshot`, `waybar`, and `check`.
- DeepInfra `CallTimeout` default 12 s.
- The "slow to answer" message states the actual cap (`>12 s`).
- While in `deepinfra.go`: compare `remaining` in whole cents (`math.Round(x*100)`) so `-8.0 − 7.97` is `3` cents (prepaid) and `-8.0 − 8.0` is exactly zero, never a float artefact; add both cases to the mapping test.
