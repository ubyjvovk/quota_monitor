## Goal
Field report from the owner's Omarchy machine: the widget sits in the bar's
right section but the dropdown still opens screen-centered. `Panel.qml`'s
`KeyboardPanel` hard-codes `centerOnBar: true`, overriding the `anchorItem`
(the icon button) that `BarWidget.injectPanel` already provides. The panel
must open at its icon and center only when it has no anchor.

## Design — implement exactly this
In the `KeyboardPanel` block: `centerOnBar: root.anchorItem === null` (with a
one-line comment: anchored popouts follow their icon's bar section; centring
is the fallback for a missing anchor only). Nothing else changes.
